Illinois rates check: shows what changed in the state's sales tax
rates since the last files you loaded, at the general merchandise
rate, place by place.

il_rates_check.bas is the macro. Click Raw, then Ctrl A, Ctrl C.

Once:
1. Open a new blank workbook in Excel.
2. Alt F11, then Insert > Module. If the new window already has a line
   in it, Ctrl A and Delete first. Paste, then close that window.
3. File > Save As > Excel Macro-Enabled Workbook (.xlsm). Name it
   IL Rates Check and keep it with your other work files.
4. Alt F8, pick CheckIllinoisRates, Run. It makes the What changed,
   Read here, Log and Saved rates tabs and the big button, then asks
   for the files.

The rest is also on the Read here tab, word for word.

This shows what changed in Illinois's sales tax rates since the last
files you loaded: each place whose rate changed, its county, the old
rate, the new rate, and when it starts. It watches the general
merchandise rate.

When to check
Illinois posts new rates for January 1 and July 1, and sometimes
another day (August 1 was one in 2026). Once that day comes, What
changed shows a red GET THE NEW FILES line.

Getting the files
1. Go to tax.illinois.gov, Research, Tax Rates, Sales Tax Rate -
   Machine Readable Files.
2. Under Sales Tax, download the top two links: Effective (it saves as
   ordmache-current.txt) and Addendum Address File
   (ordmacha-current.txt). The second one has the rates for places
   with business districts.
3. Not the Address Specific page. That file is 2 GB and this won't
   read it.

Loading them
1. Click Check new rates file on the What changed tab. The box opens
   in Downloads.
2. Hold Ctrl, click both files, then Open. Or pick one and it asks for
   the other.
3. Save the workbook when you're done. It never saves itself.

Reading What changed
Each line is a place, or one business district in a place, whose rate
changed. Starts is the day the new rate begins.
Red: went up.
Green: went down.
Blue: new.
Gray: gone.
Yellow: something else, like a new name.
Places with business districts get a line for each district (District
R01 and so on). Those rates come from the address file.
The first time, with nothing loaded before, it shows what changed on
the files' start date.
BUSINESS DISTRICTS NOT CHECKED means the address file wasn't picked.
Load both next time.

The other tabs
Log: every change once, the first time a file showed it, with the day
it was loaded. Nothing new means a load found nothing new. The buttons
on its top row filter it.
Saved rates: the rates from the last files loaded. The next files get
checked against them. It's locked, so leave it alone.

If a box asks something
If a yellow bar says macros are disabled, click Enable Content.
If it says the files look older than last time, or cut short, or not
from the same posting, answer No and download them again.
