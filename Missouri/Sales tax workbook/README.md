# Missouri use tax workbook

What it does: the quarterly Missouri use tax worksheet, MO Use Tax
Workbook - BLANK.xlsx. Paste the month's TERSE sales report and AR sales
taxes by county report on their tabs and the RECAP tab adds up the
month and the quarter: total sales, exempt sales, taxable sales and
sales due by county and rate, the county list for the return with NO
LOCAL on the 4.225% lines, and sales with no local tax. One light on
the READ ME tab says whether the month checks out; when it is red it
says what is wrong. Plain formulas, no macro, any Excel from 2010 on.

At work: download MO Use Tax Workbook - BLANK.xlsx, save a copy for the
quarter, and type the quarter's first day on READ ME. The how-to is on
READ ME beside the light.

One-time setup: none. The county and rate list on the LISTS tab starts
with the counties and rates from the 3rd quarter 2026 worksheet; add a
pair there when the light asks for one.

Open: September 2026 is the first real month through it. Jurisdiction
codes are on the list for two counties only; type the rest on LISTS as
they come up.
