MO Rates Check - setup

WHAT IT DOES
Each quarter the Missouri DOR posts a boundary file (every address range in
the state and its jurisdiction code) and a jurisdiction and total rate file.
This Access database keeps the last two quarters and shows where the use tax
rate changed, which ship-tos have the wrong rate in the system, and the ones
to look up on the state's map.

How to use it is on its main screen, in the panel at the right: where to get
the state's two files each quarter, and every step after. This page is only
for setting it up, once.

MORatesCheck.bas is the code. Open it on the repo page, click Raw, then
Ctrl A, Ctrl C.

SETUP, ONCE
1. Open Access. Blank database. Name it MO Rates Check. Create.
2. Press Alt+F11. In that window: Insert > Module.
3. Press Ctrl+A, then paste the whole MORatesCheck.bas over it.
4. Press Ctrl+S and name it MORatesCode.
5. Click anywhere inside "Sub SetUp" near the top and press F5.
   Wait for "Set up is done."
6. Close that window. Close Access and open the database again. If a yellow
   bar says some content is disabled, click Enable Content.
7. The MO Rates Check form opens. Follow How to use it on the right, from
   step 1. The first time, load this quarter and the one before.
