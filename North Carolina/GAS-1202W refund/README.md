# North Carolina GAS-1202W refund

The October 2026 GAS-1202W OCT worksheet with the month's own numbers,
in two copies, each with a sample refund of 10,000 gallons of undyed
diesel in it. The refund is the tax we'd get back when we had to pay
someone for fuel. NC hasn't said in writing where a refund goes on the
return. By phone, NC said to put it in the adjustments for now.
GAS-1202W OCT Line 28 is the worksheet's adjustment, and it goes on
GAS-1202 Line 19.

- NC GAS-1202W - October 2026 - refund as Line 29.xlsx leaves the
  worksheet as it is. The 10,000 gallons' dollars sit under GAS-1202W
  OCT Line 28 as GAS-1202W OCT Line 29, a line NC doesn't have, typed as
  a minus: 9,900 gallons after the 1% tare on GAS-1202W OCT Line 16,
  times $0.41, is $4,059.00. It goes on top of GAS-1202W OCT Line 28.
- NC GAS-1202W - October 2026 - refund flows through.xlsx puts the
  10,000 gallons on GAS-1202W OCT Line 13, Undyed Diesel (E22), the
  cell in red. GAS-1202W OCT Lines 15, 16, 17, 22 and 25 carry them to
  GAS-1202W OCT Line 28.

With the sample, both come to $28,947.57 for GAS-1202 Line 19. With no
refund, it's $33,006.57.

In the flow-through copy, GAS-1202W OCT Line 13 no longer ties to
GAS-1202 Line 7 less GAS-1202W OCT Line 1, by the 10,000 sample
gallons. The inspection tax lines, GAS-1202W OCT Lines 19, 20, 21 and
23, move with the gallons too, but they don't reach GAS-1202W OCT Line
28.

No macro. Sheet1 is the worksheet, Parts 1 to 3. Sheet2 has each
schedule's month total and its October 1 to 9 loads; October 10 to 31
is the rest. Sheet1's gallons are typed from Sheet2.

## Use it

1. Open either file. Sheet1 is the worksheet.
2. Refund as Line 29: type the real refund's dollars over the -4,059 in
   I40, as a minus. GAS-1202W OCT Line 28 (I39) plus GAS-1202W OCT
   Line 29 (I40) goes on GAS-1202 Line 19.
3. Refund flows through: click E22, the red cell, and put the real
   refund gallons in place of the 10000 in =699786+30107+10000.
   GAS-1202W OCT Line 28 (I39) goes on GAS-1202 Line 19.

Keep the GAS-1202W OCT worksheet with the month's records. NC says not
to file it with the return.
