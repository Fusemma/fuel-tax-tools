# North Carolina GAS-1202W refund

The October 2026 GAS-1202W OCT worksheet with the month's own numbers,
in two copies, each with a sample refund of $1,000 in it. The refund is
tax we paid a seller on fuel and are getting back. NC hasn't said in
writing where a refund goes on the return. By phone, NC said to put it
in the adjustments for now. GAS-1202W OCT Line 28 is the worksheet's
adjustment, and it goes on GAS-1202 Line 19.

- NC GAS-1202W - October 2026 - refund as Line 29.xlsx leaves the
  worksheet as it is. The refund sits under GAS-1202W OCT Line 28 as
  GAS-1202W OCT Line 29, a line NC doesn't have, typed as a minus. It
  goes on top of GAS-1202W OCT Line 28.
- NC GAS-1202W - October 2026 - refund flows through.xlsx takes the
  refund off GAS-1202W OCT Line 25, the cell in red, so GAS-1202W OCT
  Line 28 carries it.

With the $1,000 sample, both come to $32,006.57 for GAS-1202 Line 19.
With no refund, it's $33,006.57.

No macro. Sheet1 is the worksheet, Parts 1 to 3. Sheet2 has each
schedule's month total and its October 1 to 9 loads; October 10 to 31
is the rest. Sheet1's gallons are typed from Sheet2.

## Use it

1. Open either file. Sheet1 is the worksheet.
2. Refund as Line 29: type the real refund over the -1,000 in I40, as a
   minus. GAS-1202W OCT Line 28 (I39) plus GAS-1202W OCT Line 29 (I40)
   goes on GAS-1202 Line 19.
3. Refund flows through: click I36, the red cell, and put the real
   refund in place of the 1000 in =I31-1000. GAS-1202W OCT Line 28
   (I39) goes on GAS-1202 Line 19.

Keep the GAS-1202W OCT worksheet with the month's records. NC says not
to file it with the return.
