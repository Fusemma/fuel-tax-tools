# North Carolina GAS-1202W refund

The October 2026 GAS-1202W OCT worksheet with the month's own numbers,
in two copies, each with a sample refund of $1,000 in it. The refund is
tax we paid a seller on fuel and are getting back. NC hasn't said yet
where a refund goes on the return. By phone on Nov 18, 2026, NC said to
put it in the adjustments for now, and the worksheet's last line is the
adjustment: GAS-1202W OCT Line 28 goes on GAS-1202 Line 19.

- NC GAS-1202W OCT - refund as Line 29.xlsx leaves the worksheet as it
  is. The refund comes after Line 28 as Line 29, a minus. "Adjustment
  with refund" under it adds Line 28 and Line 29.
- NC GAS-1202W OCT - refund through the worksheet.xlsx takes the refund
  off Line 25, the cell in red, so Line 28 carries it.

With the $1,000 sample, both come to $32,006.57 for GAS-1202 Line 19.
With no refund, it's $33,006.57.

No macro. Sheet1 is the worksheet, Parts 1 to 3. Sheet2 has each
schedule's month total and its October 1 to 9 loads; October 10 to 31
is the rest. Sheet1's gallons are typed from Sheet2.

## Use it

1. Open either file. Sheet1 is the worksheet.
2. Refund as Line 29: type the real refund over the -1,000 in I40, as a
   minus. "Adjustment with refund", in I41, goes on GAS-1202 Line 19.
3. Refund through the worksheet: click I36, the red cell, and put the
   real refund in place of the 1000 in =I31-1000. Line 28, in I39, goes
   on GAS-1202 Line 19.

Keep the GAS-1202W OCT worksheet with the month's records. NC says not
to file it with the return.
