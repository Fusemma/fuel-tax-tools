# Clear diesel sales tax

North Carolina sales tax on clear diesel and gasoline, and the off-highway refund, as of Nov. 4, 2026.

Motor fuel taxed by the gallon is exempt from North Carolina sales tax, except fuel for which one of four excise refunds is allowed, the off-highway refund among them [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-164.13(11)a.]. The excise is collected from the supplier, becomes part of the cost of the fuel, and is paid by those who purchase and consume the fuel [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.62]. The off-highway refund goes to the person who purchases and uses the fuel for a purpose other than to operate a licensed highway vehicle, for the excise tax paid [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.106(d)]. The refund is the excise rate less the sales and use tax due on the fuel [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.106(d)]. The 2026 GAS-1201Q multiplies by $0.410 for the refund and by $0.232 for the sales tax due on nonhighway gallons [N.C. Dep't of Revenue, Form GAS-1201Q, 2026].

The rack. "An excise tax at the motor fuel rate is imposed on motor fuel that is: (1) Removed from a refinery or a terminal and, upon removal, is subject to the federal excise tax imposed by § 4081 of the Code." [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.81]. For 2026, "the Secretary has determined that the motor fuels and alternative fuels excise tax rate for the period of January 1, 2026, through December 31, 2026, will be 41.0 cents per gallon or gallon equivalent" [N.C. Dep't of Revenue, Motor Fuels and Alternative Fuels Tax Rates Announcements (read Nov. 3, 2026)].

Who bears it. "The tax is collected from the supplier or importer of the fuel because this method is the most efficient way to collect the tax. The tax is designed, however, to be paid ultimately by the person who consumes the fuel. The tax becomes a part of the cost of the fuel and is consequently paid by those who subsequently purchase and consume the fuel." [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.62].

The exemption. "Motor fuel, as taxed in Article 36C of this Chapter, except motor fuel for which a refund of the per gallon excise tax is allowed under G.S. 105-449.105A, G.S. 105-449.106(c), G.S. 105-449.106(d), or G.S. 105-449.107." [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-164.13(11)a.].

The refund. "A person who purchases and uses motor fuel for a purpose other than to operate a licensed highway vehicle may receive a quarterly refund for the excise tax paid during the preceding calendar quarter." and "The amount of refund allowed is the tax rate in effect under G.S. 105-449.80 for the time period less the amount of sales and use tax due on the fuel under this Chapter." [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.106(d)].

The sales tax cut. "Article 5 of Subchapter I of this Chapter determines the amount of State sales and use tax to be deducted from a motor fuel excise tax refund" [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.107A]. The Department: "G.S. 105-449.107A requires the off-highway refund to be reduced by the amount of sales tax due on the fuel." [N.C. Dep't of Revenue, GAS-1201Q instructions, Web 3-23].

The 2026 form. The refund line reads "(Multiply Line 4 by $0.410)". Line 9 is "Total gallons of motor fuel used in nonhighway equipment for which sales tax is due", and Line 10 is "Sales tax due (Multiply Line 9 by $0.232)". Line 11 is "Total gallons of motor fuel used in commercial fishing, commercial logging, railroads, farming, ocean-going vessels, and manufacturing for which no sales tax is due" [N.C. Dep't of Revenue, Form GAS-1201Q, 2026].
