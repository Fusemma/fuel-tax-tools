# North Carolina sales tax call

North Carolina sales tax on clear diesel during the motor fuel tax suspension: questions for the Department, as of Nov. 4, 2026.

Call the Excise Tax Division at 1-877-308-9092 or 919-707-7500, 8:00 to 4:30 Eastern, Monday to Friday. Ask them questions 1 to 5. Then ask to be sent to Sales and Use Tax for 6 to 18. If the transfer drops, call 1-877-252-3052 and ask for Sales and Use Tax. If anyone won't answer by phone, write that down and ask question 18.

## Each person

Get their name and ID first. Give our FEIN and the account number for their tax. Say you're asking for specific advice for the company, and the company will rely on it. Ask them to note the call and their answers on our account.

Their answer protects us only from a penalty or added assessment caused by wrong advice. It also needs their records to show the advice, our reliance on it, and full and accurate facts from us [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-264(b)]. If they say we owed the tax and they're right, nothing protects us.

Say the setup once to each person: "The fuel is clear diesel bought at the terminal from Oct. 10 to Nov. 30, at zero cents excise. The customer runs it off-highway, not in a licensed vehicle, and has no sales tax exemption of its own."

## Excise Tax Division

1. Can that customer get any off-highway refund on those zero-cent gallons? [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.106(d)]
2. How does a customer fill out the fourth-quarter GAS-1201Q when some gallons came in at zero cents? The 2026 form prints 41 cents for every quarter.
3. Can a power takeoff truck claim its yearly GAS-1200C refund on zero-cent gallons?
4. On an October GAS-1210 kerosene refund, which rate goes on which gallons? October had 41 cents through the 9th and zero after.
5. When will the FAQ the Oct. 12 notice promised be out? Can you pass this question to whoever is writing it?

## Sales and Use Tax

6. Is that sale exempt from sales tax, or taxable? What's that based on? [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-164.13(11)a.; S.L. 2026-62, Sec. 1(b)]
7. If they say nothing changed: before Oct. 10 the sales tax came out of the customer's refund. With no refund, who owes it: us, the customer, or no one?
8. Is that the same for gasoline, and for undyed kerosene, run off-highway?
9. Is it the same for special mobile equipment, the kind that files the GAS-1201ME?

Ask 10 to 13 only if they said taxable, or named someone who owes it.

10. Do we charge it on our invoice, or does the customer pay use tax?
11. Which sales do we charge it on: only ones we know are off-highway, or every clear sale unless the customer says on-road?
12. If some was already sold since Oct. 10 with no sales tax, what happens with those sales now?
13. Would there be penalty, interest, both, or neither?

Ask the rest either way.

14. Zero-cent fuel bought by Nov. 30, then sold Dec. 1 or later for on-road use: exempt or taxable? And for off-highway use?
15. With the 41 cents paid, before Oct. 10 or from Dec. 1: is the 23.2 cents the GAS-1201Q takes out of an off-highway refund the sales tax? Or does the seller charge it too? [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-449.106(d)]
16. If that customer never files the refund, who owes the sales tax?
17. A customer picks up taxable fuel, like dyed, at a terminal that isn't ours. Which county's rate do we charge? [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-164.4B(a)]

## Before you hang up

Read back their main answers so their notes match yours.

18. Can you email me that answer in writing? If not, how do I ask for a private letter ruling, and can the fee be waived? The fee runs $100 to $5,000, and the Secretary may waive it [N.C. Gen. Stat. § 105-264(d)].

Write down each person's name, ID, call number and the time.

**If they say off-highway clear at zero cents is taxable, tell the salesperson today.**

## Open

Whether off-highway clear bought at zero cents is exempt or taxable. The Department hasn't said.
