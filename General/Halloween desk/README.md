# Halloween desk

What it does: a Teams call on hold with the IRS for 865 hours, printed
to cover the HP V241 at work. Four letter pages, landscape, that tape
together into one sheet about 20 9/16 x 11 9/16 in.

At work: download the PDF and print all four pages in color at Actual
size, not Fit. Measure the screen first. Cut the white border off each
page, then tape them up by the label on each: 1 top left, 2 top right,
3 bottom left, 4 bottom right.
