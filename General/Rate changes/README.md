# Rate changes, Dec. 1, 2026 and Jan. 1, 2027

As of Nov. 27, 2026 · Sources read Oct. 13 to Nov. 25, 2026 · Open: one, at the bottom · Sources at the bottom

New York's petroleum business tax goes up Jan. 1, 2027: gasoline 0.158 to 0.165, highway diesel 0.1405 to 0.1475 [NY Pub. 908]. Quarterly filers start the new rates Mar. 1, 2027 [NY Pub. 908]. Five New York counties change or extend their local sales tax on fuel Dec. 1, 2026: Chemung, Oswego outside the city and Seneca go to 4%, Onondaga goes to 0.16, and Dutchess keeps 0.11 [NY Pub. 718-F]. South Carolina's page now names only North Carolina for exported fuel, not Georgia [SC DOR, Motor Fuel Requirements and FAQs].

## South Carolina fuel exported to Georgia

South Carolina's page now says suppliers "are not required to pay South Carolina Motor Fuel Tax on motor fuel that is exported to North Carolina," while North Carolina's suspension runs, through Nov. 30, 2026 [SC DOR, Motor Fuel Requirements and FAQs]. In October the same page said "exported to Georgia or North Carolina," with Georgia's suspension then dated Sept. 29 to Oct. 29, 2026 [SC DOR, Motor Fuel Requirements and FAQs, read Oct. 14, 2026].

## Open
