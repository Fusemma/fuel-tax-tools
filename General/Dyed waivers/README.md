# Dyed waivers

What it does: one workbook, Dyed Waivers.xlsx, that shows where every
state stands on dyed diesel on the road, as of Oct 15, 2026. Federal
comes first, then one row per state read so far, A to Z: whether dyed on
the road is waived or its enforcement paused, whether the tax on those
gallons is still owed, whether the seller is covered, the window, the
order, letter or notice itself with its number and date, and the day
its official page was read. Today works out from each row's own dates
whether it is in effect, ends within 14 days, has ended or has not
started yet, and the row turns green, amber or grey to match. The line
under the title counts the states in effect today and the ones ending
soon, and gives the federal window. The No order found tab lists the
states heard of only in the press.

At work: download Dyed Waivers.xlsx and open it. If Excel opens it in
Protected View, click Enable Editing so Today counts from today's date.

One-time setup: none. No macro.

Open: the eight states on No order found, each until an official page
turns up. Mississippi is press only too: the Agriculture Commissioner's
press statement, with the Governor's own notice not found. Thirteen
states and DC are on neither tab yet; the No order found tab names
them. Unclear means the state's own papers don't settle it yet. Who is
covered differs by order, so read the order before relying on a row. Each row is as of its Checked date, Oct 12 to 15,
2026; a change after that is not on it.
