Combined exemption certificate: IRS Model Certificates M and P joined
into one form, the way most suppliers send it. Source: IRS Publication
510 (Rev. December 2025), appendix page 53 (Model M: gasoline and
aviation gasoline, vendor claim under IRC 6416(a)(4)) and page 56
(Model P: diesel fuel and kerosene for state or local government use,
vendor claim under IRC 6427). p510.pdf in this folder, pulled from
irs.gov Sep 29, 2026.

Each line under the marker is one line or paragraph of the form.
Lines starting with * join the two models' wording (the fuel lists,
the two code sections with "and/or", M's numbering with P's words) or
add the one limit below. test_exemption_template.py checks every other
line word for word against Model M or P. ________ is a fill-in blank,
[ ] a box.

The one limit: the nonprofit school option says "(gasoline or aviation
gasoline only)". For diesel or kerosene a nonprofit school makes its
own claim (Pub 510 page 15 lists it as an ultimate purchaser use), and
the vendor's diesel claim covers state or local government use only
(page 21, Model P).

The vendor must hold the certificate when it claims. A certificate
expires at the earlier of 1 year after its date or the date the buyer
gives a new one (page 21).
