# Forms templates

What it does: Word templates for two IRS certificates, and for the letter
that goes with a Form 843 claim. The 637 notification certificate
(Notification Certificate - TEMPLATE.docx) and the federal excise
exemption certificate (Exemption Certificate - TEMPLATE.docx, Models M
and P in one form). Every fill-in spot is a click-and-type field with a
gray hint; signature and date stay blank for ink. The wording follows IRS
Pub 510 (Rev. December 2025).

The Form 843 explanation (Form 843 Explanation - TEMPLATE.docx) is the
written explanation mailed with a Form 843 claim. It is three pages and
an exhibit cover sheet: a cover page that makes the ask, then five short
sections. One timeline table holds every notice and step, the money is a
three-line table, and the exhibits are a list. A one-line gray hint under
each heading says what goes there; delete it when the section is done.
Type the company name, EIN, tax period and amount once and every other
spot fills in, the header too.

Setup: download the .docx, fill the fields, print. The Word cheat sheet
beside this file says how the fields behave. For the Form 843 exhibits,
make one cover sheet per exhibit: click just before "Exhibit" on the last
page, press Ctrl+Shift+End, then Ctrl+C. Press Ctrl+End, then Enter, then
Ctrl+V. Each copy starts on its own page.
